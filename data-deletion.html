Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 22, 2026

You can ask Mesvy to delete your data at any time. The steps depend on whether you are a business that connected an Instagram account, or a person who messaged a business that uses Mesvy.

Businesses that connected an Instagram account

  1. Remove Mesvy's access in Instagram: open Instagram → Settings and activity → Website permissions → Apps and websites → find Mesvy → Remove.
  2. Send an email to info@mesvy.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and your Instagram username.
  3. We delete your account data, access tokens and conversation history within 30 days and confirm by email.

People who messaged a business that uses Mesvy

  1. Send an email to info@mesvy.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include your Instagram username and the name of the business you messaged.
  3. We delete your messages and any contact details stored by Mesvy within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data that the business has already copied into its own systems (for example, its CRM) is controlled by that business; please contact the business directly as well.
