AI assistant for business messaging

Every customer message answered in seconds

Mesvy connects to your business's professional Instagram account and replies to Direct messages and comments 24/7 in your customer's language, collects leads and sends them straight to your team.

Get started How it works

What Mesvy does

Built for clinics, shops and service businesses that get many messages and cannot afford to miss a customer.

💬

Direct replies

Answers questions in Instagram Direct instantly using your own knowledge base: prices, services, addresses, working hours.

🗨️

Comment replies

Replies to comments under your posts and Reels and continues the conversation privately when needed.

🌍

Many languages

Understands and replies in your customer's language, including Uzbek, Russian, Tajik and English.

📇

Lead capture

When a customer leaves a phone number, Mesvy creates a lead in your CRM and notifies your team in Telegram.

🛡️

Your rules

You decide what the assistant may say. Sensitive topics are handed over to a human.

📊

Reports

See how many messages were answered, how fast, and how many leads were collected.

How it works

Setup takes minutes. You stay in control of your account at all times.

Connect

Log in with Instagram and allow Mesvy to manage messages and comments of your professional account.

Teach

Add information about your business: services, prices, FAQ and the tone of voice you want.

Go live

Mesvy starts answering customers. You can review conversations, take over at any time or disconnect.
