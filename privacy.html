Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mesvy ("Mesvy", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when businesses use our service to automate replies in Instagram Direct messages and comments, and when people send messages to those businesses.

Mesvy is operated by Individual Entrepreneur ABDURAUFOV FAXRIDDIN KODIRJON O'G'LI, Kamarsada street 88, Chust, Namangan region, 161100, Uzbekistan. Contact: info@mesvy.com.

1. Who this policy applies to

  • Business customers — companies that connect their professional Instagram account to Mesvy.
  • End users — people who send messages or leave comments on a business's Instagram account that uses Mesvy.

2. Information we collect

When a business connects its Instagram account through Meta's official login, we receive, with the business's permission:

  • Basic profile information of the business account: account ID, username, name and profile picture.
  • Messages sent to the business in Instagram Direct and the replies sent by the business.
  • Comments on the business's posts and Reels and the replies to them.
  • Access tokens issued by Meta that allow us to send replies on behalf of the business.

About end users we process only what they send to the business: their Instagram-scoped user ID, username, the content of their messages and comments, and any contact details they choose to share (for example, a phone number).

From business customers we also collect account information such as name, email address and the business knowledge base they upload.

3. How we use information

  • To generate and send replies to messages and comments on behalf of the business.
  • To pass leads (for example, a phone number left by a customer) to the business's CRM or Telegram, as configured by the business.
  • To show the business conversation history and statistics.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use end-user data for advertising. We do not use Instagram data for any purpose other than providing the service to the business that connected the account.

4. AI processing

To compose replies, message text and the business's knowledge base may be processed by third-party AI model providers (such as OpenAI or Anthropic) under their API terms, which do not allow them to use this data to train their models.

5. Sharing

We share information only with:

  • the business whose account received the message;
  • service providers that help us run Mesvy (cloud hosting, AI model providers), bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • authorities, when required by applicable law.

6. Data retention

We keep conversation data while the business account is connected to Mesvy. When a business disconnects its account or deletes its Mesvy account, we delete its Instagram data within 30 days, except where we are required by law to keep it longer.

7. Security

Access tokens and data are stored on protected servers with restricted access. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Your rights and data deletion

You can request access to, correction of or deletion of your data at any time. See our Data Deletion instructions or write to info@mesvy.com. We respond within 30 days.

9. Children

Mesvy is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be communicated to business customers by email.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@mesvy.com.
